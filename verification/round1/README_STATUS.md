# Round-1 forms: status

The 477 round-1 forms (`review_AG`, `review_MO`, `packets.html`) show each pair as it was prepared for a repeated experiment that was not run: every content field of both excerpts (including `safety_guardrails`, `applicability` and `exclusions_or_cautions`, which no original runner serialized) and the full vignette. That is more than any of the five original runner configurations presented to the models. The sentence in `README_REVIEWERS.md`, "The text in the *_as_shown_to_model columns is exactly what the evaluated models receive.", referred to that unrun experiment, not to the original evaluations.

The R2 reanalysis uses the physicians' answers for the 333 analyzed pairs (the pairs whose authentic and modified excerpts differ in content visible to the models in the original runs), descriptively: `verification/round1_summary.json`, `verification/round1_summary.csv` and `verification/round1_analyzed_pairs.csv`. No pair is excluded on these answers. For these pairs, the excerpt text and the visible metadata shown in the forms equal the original as-run excerpts (333 of 333 authentic, 293 of 333 modified); the 40 fabricated_citation modified excerpts show the neutral DOI suffix 10.9999/cg.<year>.NNNN instead of the 10.9999/fake.NNNN that the models saw.
